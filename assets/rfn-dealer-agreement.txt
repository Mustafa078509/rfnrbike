                                   DEALER AGREEMENT


Qualification of Becoming an Authorized Dealer:

1. Dealer agrees that his/her business:
    A. Is a retail/wholesale operation in a commercially zoned business area.
    B. That the business operates on a full-time basis (opens a minimum of five days a week).
    C. Has a business phone number that is listed with directory assistance.
    D. Has any/all license(s) required for the state/county/city (business license, DMV dealer
       license, etc.).
    E. Has own full service & repair departments to handle any/all service(s), repair(s), and
       warranty issue(s).

2. Upon signing the agreement, the Dealer must provide legible copies of:

         A. Business License.
         B. State Retail Certificate.
         C. Dealer License
         D. Certificate/Proof of Insurance (Product Liability, General Liability, and any other
            insurance as required by state and/or federal law). E. Photos of retail store

Dealer’s Responsibilities, Representations, and Warranties:

1. Dealer agrees that there will be a showroom, displaying & safely storing Distributor’s
   products.

2. Dealer agrees to have a full service and repair department with personnel trained and
   competent in the service and repairs at the retail location.

3. Dealer agrees to exert its best efforts in the promotion, retail sales, and service of the products.
   Dealer shall not make any misrepresentations or misleading statements regarding the
   products. Dealer recognizes that retail customer has the right to purchase Distributor’s
   products without being required to purchase any optional equipment or accessories unless the
   equipment or accessories are required under applicable laws or regulations.

4. Dealer must carry appropriate and sufficient policies of insurance and which, must comply
   with all statutory regulations in the state. Furthermore, the Dealer shall be solely responsible
   for the payment(s) of all deductibles on its own policies.

5. Dealer Must Maintain MAP for each unit. The MAP will be provided to the dealer on a
   separate form attached.

6. Dealer agrees to the warranty policy provided by the Distributor by purchasing from the
   Distributor.

7. If Dealer needs to contact the Distributor to resolve customer issue(s), the Dealer will be the
   SOLE CONTACT with the Distributor.


Marketing and Promotional Programs:

1. Dealer shall target appropriate customers in its marketing of Distributor’s products.
2. Dealer shall develop and actively utilize programs for the marketing and promotion of the
   Distributor’s products that Dealer offers to the public.
3. Dealer shall also cooperate fully with any/all promotional programs developed by the
   Distributor.

Indemnification:

   Dealer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Distributor, its officers, directors, employees, and
   agents from and against any and all third-party claims, liabilities, damages, losses, and expenses
   (including reasonable attorney’s fees) to the extent arising out of or resulting from:
   1. Dealer’s negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct;
   2. Dealer’s breach of this Agreement;
   3. Dealer’s improper assembly, installation, maintenance, repair, modification, or use of the products;
   4. Dealer’s failure to provide adequate instructions, warnings, or safety information to its customers,
       where such obligation is expressly required by Distributor in writing; or
   5. Dealer’s violation of applicable laws or regulations in connection with the sale or distribution of the
       products.

   Notwithstanding the foregoing, Dealer shall have no obligation to indemnify, defend, or hold
   harmless Distributor for any claims, damages, or liabilities to the extent arising from:
    Any defect in the design, manufacture, or labeling of the products not caused by Dealer;
    Distributor’s or manufacturer’s negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct; or
    Any modification or misuse of the products not performed or authorized by Dealer.
   Distributor shall promptly notify Dealer of any claim subject to indemnification and shall reasonably
   cooperate in the defense. Dealer shall have the right to control the defense and settlement of any such

   claim, provided that no settlement imposing liability or obligations on Distributor may be entered
   into without Distributor’s prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
   Termination:
1. Unless otherwise noted, this agreement shall remain in full force and effect for a term of one
   (1) Year. Thereafter, this agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms.

2. Either party can terminate this agreement, without cause, by giving thirty (30) days
   WRITTEN NOTICE delivered to the other party (proof of delivery is recommended).
