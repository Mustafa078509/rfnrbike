RFN PARTS + SUPABASE CONNECTION
===============================

The following catalog pages now load price and stock from the Supabase products table:
- ares-2026-parts.html
- ares-parts.html
- rally-parts.html
- warrior-sx-e5-parts.html
- warrior-sx-e8-parts.html
- warrior-sx-700-parts.html
- evo-20-parts.html

How it works
------------
1. Each page keeps its original diagrams and part layout.
2. /parts-database.js calls /api/store-products?product_type=part.
3. Parts are matched by part_number.
4. Dealer price, retail price, product name and stock_quantity come from Supabase.
5. stock_quantity = 0 disables Add to Cart and shows Out of Stock.
6. Low stock uses low_stock_threshold.
7. The Supabase product UUID is saved with the cart item for order/inventory processing.

Required Netlify environment variables
--------------------------------------
SUPABASE_URL
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY
ADMIN_EMAILS

Important
---------
All part records must use product_type = 'part' and their part_number must match the part code shown in the HTML catalog.
Do not place SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY in admin/config.js or any browser file.
