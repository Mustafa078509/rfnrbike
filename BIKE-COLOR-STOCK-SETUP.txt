RFN BIKE COLOR AVAILABILITY

1) Deploy this ZIP to Netlify.
2) In Supabase Dashboard > SQL Editor, run:
   supabase/add-bike-color-status.sql
3) Open Admin > Bikes.

What changed:
- Add Bike form in Admin > Bikes.
- Each bike color has its own In Stock / Out of Stock status.
- If every color is Out of Stock, the bike saves as Out of Stock (unless Pre-Order or Hidden).
- If any color is In Stock, the bike saves as In Stock (unless Pre-Order or Hidden).
- Bike Dealer Price, Retail Price and Quantity remain editable.
- Parts system was not changed.
- index.html was not changed.
